About Me

I grew up in Southern Utah and have always valued connection, family, and community. My passion for helping others began in high school and has grown into a professional commitment to supporting individuals through difficult experiences. I strive to create a safe, supportive environment where clients feel understood and empowered to grow.

Counseling Philosophy & Approach

I believe that seeking help is a courageous step. My goal is to create a non-judgmental and supportive space where clients can explore their thoughts, emotions, and experiences freely.

My approach is integrative, grounded in Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), while incorporating person-centered, trauma-informed care, and elements of ACT and DBT. I prioritize building a strong therapeutic relationship while using evidence-based interventions to support meaningful and lasting change.
