Counseling Philosophy

Asking for help can be difficult, but meaningful change begins with that step. My approach is warm, non-judgmental, and rooted in building a strong therapeutic relationship. I aim to create a safe space where clients feel heard, understood, and empowered.

My theoretical orientation is integrative, grounded in Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) while incorporating person-centered and trauma-informed approaches. I also draw from ACT and DBT techniques to support emotional awareness and regulation.
